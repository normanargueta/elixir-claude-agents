# Northwind Analytics - Customer Handbook (excerpt)

## Plans

Northwind Analytics sells three subscription tiers: Free, Pro, and Enterprise.
The Free plan includes 1 project and community support only. The Pro plan
includes 10 projects, email support with a 1 business-day response target, and
API access. The Enterprise plan is custom-priced and adds SSO, a dedicated
success manager, and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Billing and refunds

Pro subscriptions are billed monthly or annually. Annual plans are discounted by
two months compared to paying monthly.

The refund window for the Pro plan is 30 days from the date of the first
payment. Refunds are not available for renewal charges, only for the initial
purchase. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period; we do
not prorate downgrades.

Billing disputes should first be raised with support@northwind.example. If the
dispute is not resolved within five business days, it is escalated to the
Finance Operations team, reachable at finance-ops@northwind.example. Chargebacks
filed before contacting support will result in immediate account suspension
pending review.

## Support channels

Free plan: community forum only.
Pro plan: email support at support@northwind.example, 1 business-day target.
Enterprise plan: shared Slack channel plus a named success manager.

Security issues on any plan go to security@northwind.example and are triaged
within 24 hours.

## Data retention

Project data is retained for the life of the subscription plus 60 days after
cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted. Customers can request an
export at any time from Settings > Data, which produces a downloadable archive
within one hour.
